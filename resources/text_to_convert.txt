`!help` or `/help`: this command! lists out all currently available commands 
`/setup`: lets you configure some bot settings

`!settings`: shows the bot's current configuration

`!flip`, `/flip`, or `!coinflip`: flips a coin

`!metricthis`: converts the units in the previous message to metric

`/story`: hear a story

`/do_you_have_a_map`: ask if we have a map
